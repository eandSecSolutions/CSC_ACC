# Slide 03 — creative and interaction brief

## Intended executive message

Four operating environments need specialist systems, but share an operating picture, clear decisions and accountable coordination. The command center supervises missions and exceptions. It does not replace immediate safety decisions made inside each asset.

## Visual approach

Use four distinct, premium UAE operating environments instead of a generic network diagram. Each photograph contains detailed, naturally lit equipment already integrated into the composition. Produce a matched day/night pair for every domain. Keep the photograph large and stable while a separate console explains its operational meaning.

Retain navy, warm metallic gold and restrained aqua from the presentation shell. Image transitions use GSAP. Sparse particles, softly varying sky illumination, navigation lights, a turn indicator and localized water shimmer bring the setting to life without covering the content. No photographic vehicle is cut out and floated across the image, and no synthetic model is composited over the artwork.

## Interaction sequence

| Domain   | Challenge                                           | Decision                                             | Outcome                                      |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Air      | Conflicting flight intent at a shared crossing      | Review and approve separate corridors                | Two flights continue at separated levels     |
| Land     | A maintenance closure blocks a robot patrol         | Review and approve the available service-lane bypass | Inspection continues around the closure      |
| Sea      | A patrol approaches the authorized mission boundary | Review and approve an inward return leg              | Coverage continues inside the permitted area |
| Mobility | A pedestrian occupies the crossing                  | Explain the vehicle's approved local yield policy    | The vehicle resumes after clearance          |

The audience first chooses a domain, then runs its example. A short challenge explanation leads to a clear review button. Route changes explicitly wait for approval. The mobility button is labelled as watching a safety sequence, not remotely approving driving behavior. The control inset is marked illustrative and shows exactly the maneuver discussed in the text.

## Layout and usability contract

- The image stage and phase dock keep identical geometry across all four tabs and all mission phases.
- The decision button stays in the console footer, outside the scrolling text area.
- Stage labels and photographic context stay visible while the inset animates.
- Tab buttons support arrows, Home and End. Active/selected state is exposed to assistive technology.
- English and Arabic content use the same photograph and scene coordinates. Logos and global controls keep their physical shell positions.
- Pause, hidden-tab suspension, reduced motion and disposal apply to both ambient effects and mission animation.
- All resources run from the extracted local folder. No CDN, telemetry feed or server is required.

## Deliberate scope

These scenes explain a proposed capability. They are not an operational digital twin, simulation of validated vehicle physics, approved airspace design, or live control interface. The mission inset animates simplified explanatory paths while the photographic equipment remains part of its still image. The next unfinished slide remains Command Center (04).
